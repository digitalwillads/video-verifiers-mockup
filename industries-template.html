Open question: what counts as proof?

Many of the top proofs are how-to videos for people who want to work in the industry, such as an ebook video for Books. The new channel makes explainers for outsiders, such as the documentary that proves Plastic Surgery. Will decides.

  1. Only explainers for outsiders. Claude’s pick, because it matches the channel’s videos.
  2. Any video about the industry’s business, how-to videos too. This is how the verifier works now.

Best proof first. A bar is the proof video’s views divided by its channel’s average video. The scale is logarithmic: each step is 10 times the one before. The dark line is the 3x needed to pass.

    The rules. A: the line names one clear industry (AI judgment). B: it does not repeat an earlier line (code). C: people watch its story. Code keeps only videos over 3 minutes, on channels with 1,000 or more subscribers, whose titles name the industry, with 3x or more of their channel’s average views. Then the AI checks that the video is about how the industry makes money, gets customers or works as a business.
